# Contributing

How to comment on, and propose changes to, the RFCs of the Scientific Working Group (SWG) on Responsible AI for IAM Research (AI4IAM), starting with [RFC 0001](rfc-0001.md).

Everything posted is public and permanent: do not post confidential material or others' unpublished results or personal data. Contributions are accepted under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). Comments are accepted through GitHub only.

## 1. Comment (issues)

1. Search the [issues](https://github.com/AI4IAM/AI4IAM-guidelines/issues); if one covers your point, comment there.
2. Otherwise open a **New issue** titled with the section number and a summary, e.g. `2.2: labeling of AI-generated scenarios`.
3. Quote the passage, explain the concern, propose a change if you have one, give references, and state any relevant interest.

The co-chairs acknowledge each issue within 7 working days.

## 2. Propose wording (pull requests)

Open an issue first for anything beyond an editorial fix. Use the pencil icon to edit a file on GitHub, or submit a pull request.

## 3. Review and decisions

1. The co-chairs classify each change as *editorial* (no change in meaning), merged after one review, or *substantive*.
2. Substantive changes stay open for comment for at least 14 days and are announced through the AI4IAM SWG mailing list.
3. Decisions are made by rough consensus, as judged by the co-chairs, not by vote. Every objection gets either a text change or a stated reason, recorded in the pull request.
4. Unresolved questions go to the open-questions file (for RFC 0001, [rfc-0001-open-questions.md](rfc-0001-open-questions.md)) or stay as open issues for the next review.
5. A contributor may ask the co-chairs to reconsider a decision within 14 days; remaining disagreement is referred to the IAMC Scientific Steering Committee (SSC).

## 4. Versions and review

Numbered versions are released with a dated Document History entry and a DOI; cite those, not the working draft. Version 1.0 follows the first full comment period. Each RFC is reviewed in full at least once a year, or earlier on request via an issue.

## 5. Conduct

Discussion follows the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/). Comment on ideas, not people. Contributions from all regions, career stages and disciplines are welcome, including from people who use or study IAMs rather than build them.
